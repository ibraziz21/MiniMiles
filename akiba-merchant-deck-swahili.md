## On register — read this before reviewing

Rasimu 1 was pure Swahili and it read like a government circular. This version is the mixed register Kenyans actually use in business: **Swahili sentence structure, English for the words nobody translates anyway** — offer, campaign, scan, customer, till, results, set-up, data.

**Two calibrations that matter:**

**This is not Sheng.** Nairobi youth slang would land badly here. Many Mombasa merchants are older, and the coast takes real pride in speaking good Kiswahili — heavy slang reads as disrespectful, or as someone who came from Nairobi to sell them something. The register is *business-mixed*: clean Swahili with English business vocabulary, which is exactly how a shop owner and a supplier talk to each other.

**Printed copy and spoken copy are calibrated differently.** Heavy code-mixing sounds natural out loud and looks careless in print — a slide dense with English fragments reads as unprofessional even when the same sentence spoken sounds warm. So:

- **Headlines and body copy** — Swahili base, English only for technical and business terms
- **"Sema hivi" lines** — looser and more mixed, because that's how the sentence will actually leave someone's mouth

> **Still needs an hour with a Mombasa speaker before it goes to a merchant.** Not for accuracy — for register. That's the thing an outsider gets wrong, and it's the thing a merchant notices first.

# Slaidi 2 · Hakuna kinachojengeka

> ### Anaweza kurudi kesho. Anaweza asirudi. Na hutajua kwa nini.

- Hujui customers wako wa kawaida ni nani — unakumbuka sura, si tabia yao ya kununua
- Hujui nani aliacha kuja, wala lini
- Huna njia ya kumpa customer mzuri sababu ya kukuchagua tena
- Duka la jirani linauza kitu kile kile, kwa bei ile ile

Kila sale inaanza upya. **Hakuna kinachojengeka.**

Kwa sasa kinachomshikilia customer ni mazoea na location tu. **Zote mbili zinaweza kubadilika kesho.**

*Hii ni kweli — biashara ikiwa nzuri au mbaya.*

**Sema hivi:** *"Unawajua customers wako wazuri kwa sura. Lakini kama mmoja wao aliacha kuja mwezi uliopita, ungejua?"*

# Slaidi 4 · Akiba ni nini

> ### Akiba inafanya pesa ya kila siku iwe sababu ya kurudi.

Customers wako wanapata **Akiba Miles** wakinunua kwako. Wanatumia hizo Miles kwenye offers — zako, na za biashara zingine kwenye network.

Pesa ambayo walikuwa wanatumia tayari inaanza kuwarudisha kwako.

---

**Akiba si:**

- **Si payment system.** Customer anakulipa vile vile kama sasa — cash, M-Pesa, vyovyote unavyopokea pesa leo.
- **Hatugusi pesa yako.** Inakuja kwako moja kwa moja, kama kawaida.
- **Hatuchukui percentage yoyote ya sales zako.** Hata shilingi moja.
- **Miles si deni.** Si pesa unayodaiwa na mtu yeyote.

---

**Sema hivi:** *"Kabla sijaeleza inafanyaje kazi — niseme kwanza si nini, sababu watu wengi wanadhani vibaya."*

# Slaidi 5 · Inafanyaje kazi

> ### Steps nne. Hakuna kinachobadilika kwenye jinsi unavyopokea pesa.

1. **Customer anakulipa** — cash, M-Pesa, kama kawaida
2. **Anakuonyesha code yake ya Akiba** kwenye simu
3. **Una-scan na kuweka amount aliyolipa** — anapata Mile 1 kwa kila KES 100
4. **Anatumia Miles kwenye offers** — pamoja na zako

Miles zinaendelea kuongezeka kila mahali anaponunua. Ndiyo maana yake: customer anapata kutoka kwa pesa ambayo lazima atumie, halafu anatumia hizo Miles kwa vitu anavyotaka.

**Na wanaweza kukupata.** Customers wanaotafuta mahali pa kupata na kutumia Miles wanaiona biashara yako.

**Sema hivi:** *"Hakuna kinachobadilika kwenye till yako. Anakuonyesha code, una-scan na kuweka amount. Sekunde tano."*

# Slaidi 8 · Unachopata

> ### Vingine kati ya hivi vinafanya kazi tangu siku ya kwanza. Vingine vinakua na network. Hii ndiyo tofauti.

## Tangu siku ya kwanza — network ikiwa kubwa au ndogo

- **Campaign tools.** Tengeneza offer, weka rules, uone hasa nani ame-claim. *"Kuja mara 5, upate 25% off"* — wewe unaamua.
- **Data ya customers wako.** Nani ni wa kawaida. Nani aliacha kuja. Wanatumia kiasi gani, na lini.
- **Content.** Tunatengeneza video kuhusu biashara yako na offer halisi — na unapata numbers zinazomaanisha kitu: claims, scans, customers wapya. Si views.
- **Uko kwenye map.** Biashara yako inaonekana kwenye Akiba customers wanapotafuta pa kupata na kutumia Miles.
- **Signage na vifaa**, tunatoa.

## Network ikikua kwenye eneo lako

- Customers wanakuchagua **kwa sababu wanapata Miles hapa**
- **Traffic halisi kutoka kwenye listing** — watu wenye Miles, wanaotafuta pa kuzitumia
- Position dhidi ya duka la jirani ambalo halina Akiba

---

**Tunajenga sehemu ya pili kwenye mtaa wako sasa hivi. Sehemu ya kwanza inaanza siku unayojiunga.**

**Sema hivi:** *"Sitakuahidi customers kesho. Ninachoweza kukuambia ni hasa unachopata kesho, na hasa kinachokuja baadaye."*

# Slaidi 9 · Customer mmoja

> ### Fikiria customer mmoja.

Amina anakula lunch kwako karibu kila Jumanne. KES 250. Anainua simu yake, una-scan, anapata Miles kadhaa. Hakuna anayefikiria sana.

Anapata pia kwenye tuktuk. Na dukani. Na chemist. Sehemu ambazo alikuwa anaenda tayari.

Unaweka offer: **kuja mara tano, upate KES 200 off kwenye meal yako ijayo.**

Wiki tano baadaye ana-claim. **Anakuja na dada yake.**

---

**Hiki ndicho kilichobadilika kwako:**

- Unajua Amina yupo — si kama sura, bali kama customer
- Unajua anakuja kama mara moja kwa wiki
- Unajua ni **offer yako** iliyomrudisha siku ile ya Alhamisi
- Unajua dada yake ni mpya, na alitoka wapi

**Alikuwa customer usiyemjua. Sasa ni mtu unayeweza kumfanyia kitu.**

**Sema hivi:** *"Nikuambie hii inavyokuwa kwa customer mmoja."*

# Slaidi 10 · Tunachohitaji kutoka kwako

> ### Hii inafanya kazi tu ikiwa inaonekana.

- **Uliza kila customer** — *"uko kwenye Akiba?"* Sekunde tano, kila sale
- **Fundisha yeyote aliye kwenye counter** — pamoja na staff wapya. Tutarudi kufundisha tena kila unapoajiri mtu
- **Weka card** mahali customers wanapoiona — tunakupa
- **Heshimu offers unazotengeneza** — wewe unaziweka, kwa hivyo unazi-control
- **Tuambie kitu kikikwama** — tungependa kusikia mapema

---

**Ndio hiyo. Hakuna till mpya. Hakuna system mpya. Hakuna mabadiliko kwenye jinsi unavyopokea pesa.**

**Sema hivi:** *"Kuna kazi ndani yake na ni bora niseme wazi. Kubwa ni kuuliza kila customer — hiyo ndiyo inayofanya i-work au isifanye kazi."*
